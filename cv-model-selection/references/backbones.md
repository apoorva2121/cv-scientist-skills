# Backbones and foundation encoders: method inventory

Source: research R3 (verified 2026-09-27 against PyPI, the Hugging Face API, arXiv and GitHub; see the header below for method). Adaptation strategies (probe, LP-FT, LoRA, WiSE-FT, distillation) live in `cv-training/references/adaptation.md`.

Contents: key findings; A CNN backbones; B transformers and hybrids; C self-supervised and vision-language encoders (+ normalisation constants); D rankings and leaderboards; F domain foundation models (medical, pathology, remote sensing, documents, industrial); G efficient deployment backbones; decision guidance; could not verify.


Verified method inventory for the Computer Vision Scientist skill, area 3: general-purpose image encoders, how to adapt them, and which ones are safe to ship. Task-specific heads and per-task SOTA are covered in a separate area.

**Checked 2026-09-27.** Package versions come from the PyPI JSON API (upload time of the first file of the latest release): timm 1.0.30 (2026-09-22), transformers 5.17.0 (2026-09-09), open_clip_torch 3.3.0 (2026-02-27), peft 0.21.0 (2026-09-15), torch 2.14.0 and torchvision 0.29.0 (2026-09-02). timm tags and pretrained configs (`hf_hub_id`, `license`, `input_size`, `mean`/`std`) were read from an installed timm 1.0.30. transformers class names were read from an installed transformers 5.17.0, and open_clip names from the 3.3.0 wheel's `pretrained.py`. Weight licences and gating come from the Hugging Face API (`cardData.license`, `license_name`, `gated`, `createdAt`) and were cross-checked with upstream LICENSE files fetched from GitHub. Papers were checked through the arXiv API (ID, title, v1 date). "Released" means the arXiv v1 date; "(HF)" marks a Hugging Face repo creation date instead. Each accuracy number names its source: timm results CSVs, model cards, READMEs or abstracts. timm ImageNet top-1 values are the best-resolution row of `results-imagenet.csv` unless a resolution is given.

**Licence vocabulary.** *Permissive* means Apache-2.0, MIT or BSD. *Custom-commercial* means commercial use is allowed under a bespoke licence (for example field-of-use bans, terms that must be passed on, or gated access), so it needs legal review under a permissive-by-default policy. *NC* means non-commercial or research-only. Code and weights licences are listed separately because they often differ.

**Most important findings (2026-09-27)**
1. **Mirror metadata is not the licence.** Apple moved AIMv2, DFN-CLIP and MobileCLIP weights to the research-only **Apple AMLR** licence on 2025-02-28 (HF commit "AMLR license"). The timm mirrors (`timm/aimv2_*`, `timm/vit_*_clip_*.dfn*`) still say `apple-ascl` or sample-code. In the other direction, `facebook/convnextv2-*` and `facebook/vit-mae-*` are tagged Apache-2.0 on HF, but the upstream repos say their ImageNet weights are CC-BY-NC, and timm tags them `cc-by-nc-4.0`. Always follow the upstream licence, which here is also the most restrictive.
2. **DINOv3 is not permissive.** Its code and weights use the custom *DINOv3 License*. HF access is gated with manual approval. Derivatives must be distributed under the same agreement. ITAR, military, nuclear and espionage uses are banned, and Meta may amend the terms. Commercial use is not prohibited, so this is custom-commercial rather than NC.
3. **There is a strong permissive encoder for every job:** SigLIP 2 (Apache-2.0, image and text), Perception Encoder Core and Spatial (Apache-2.0), DINOv2 with registers (Apache-2.0), TIPSv2 (Apache-2.0, 2026-04) and LingBot-Vision (Apache-2.0, 2026-07). These weights are NC: Web-SSL, MetaCLIP 1 and 2, EUPE, Franca, I-JEPA, MAE, Hiera-MAE and ConvNeXt V2.
4. **A maintained frozen-feature leaderboard now exists** (billpsomas/efficient-probing, last commit 2026-09-22). ImageNet linear-probe scores: MetaCLIP 2 bigG-378 88.1, DINOv3 ViT-7B 88.0, EVA02-CLIP E-14+ 87.8, SigLIP 2 So400m 86.3. Linear probes under-read MIM and CLIP-style encoders: attentive probing lifts MAE ViT-B from 67.8 to 75.5.
5. **The evidence changes adaptation defaults.** LP-FT beats full fine-tuning by about 10% out of distribution (2202.10054). WiSE-FT adds 4-6 pp under distribution shift (2109.01903). Tuned PEFT matches full fine-tuning even with many shots (2409.16434). CLIP prompt-learning methods need per-task tuning to beat a linear probe and can fall below zero-shot under drift (2312.12730).
6. **Leaderboard landscape.** paperswithcode.com now 302-redirects to huggingface.co/papers/trending (checked 2026-09-27). timm's `results-imagenet.csv` was last updated 2025-10-30; its top entry is EVA-02-L/14 at 448 px with 90.056%. open_clip's zero-shot results CSV was last updated 2023-11-22 and omits SigLIP 2, PE and MetaCLIP 2.

## Decision guidance with citations

**Step 0: licence gate (permissive by default).**
- **Permissive shortlist:**
  - Frozen or fine-tuned: DINOv2-reg (Apache-2.0), SigLIP 2 (Apache-2.0), PE Core/Spatial/Lang (Apache-2.0), TIPSv2 (Apache-2.0), EVA-02 / EVA-CLIP / OpenCLIP LAION and DataComp (MIT), LingBot-Vision (Apache-2.0, very new).
  - Supervised: ConvNeXt V1, MobileNetV4, EfficientViT, RepViT, timm SBB ViTs, CAFormer/MambaOut (Apache/MIT).
  - Sources: sections B-C and the HF API.
- **Custom-commercial (escalate for review):** DINOv3 (incl. ConvNeXt and satellite weights), C-RADIO v3/v4 (NVIDIA Open Model License, with an NC GitHub code licence), HAI-DEF models (MedSigLIP, CXR/Derm/Path Foundation), OlmoEarth, Gemma-licensed towers, and the Apple FastViT/MobileOne licences.
- **Never ship (NC):**
  - General encoders: Apple AMLR models (AIMv2, DFN, MobileCLIP 1/2, even when a mirror says otherwise), ConvNeXt V2, MAE, Hiera-MAE, I-JEPA, Web-SSL, MetaCLIP 1/2, EUPE, Franca, SWAG/SWSL/SEER.
  - Domain models: UNI/UNI2, CONCH, TITAN, Virchow2, H-optimus-1, H0-mini, Phikon-v2, LayoutLMv3, Nougat, BiomedParse, Curia.
  - Code: VPT's code, the RADIO GitHub code, and AGPL LightlyTrain.
  - Data: models trained on MVTec AD.

**By data size (labelled images per class).**
- **Zero labels:** use zero-shot SigLIP 2 or PE-Core (85.0 and 85.4 on ImageNet at the largest sizes), or MedSigLIP for medical data under HAI-DEF terms. Then use k-NN on DINOv2 or DINOv3 features for retrieval-style labelling (sections C and E).
- **Up to about 20 per class:** use frozen features with logistic regression or k-NN. Try more than one encoder: DINOv2-reg/DINOv3 for "objects and stuff", SigLIP 2/PE for semantics nameable in text. With CLIP models, use CLAP, LP++ or a zero-shot-initialised head rather than prompt learning (2312.12730, 2404.02285). Do not fully fine-tune.
- **About 20 to 1,000 per class:**
  - Start from the linear probe and move to LP-FT (+10% OOD vs fine-tuning; 2202.10054) or LoRA via peft (similar to full fine-tuning when tuned; 2409.16434).
  - Use LLRD 0.65-0.75 (MAE recipe) and EMA.
  - Starting from a CLIP-like model, finish with WiSE-FT (+4 to 6 pp under shift; 2109.01903).
  - Average seeds or hyperparameter runs into a soup (2203.05482).
- **Over 1,000 per class, or a large domain gap:** use full fine-tuning with LLRD and EMA.
  - Given tens of thousands of unlabelled in-domain images, first continue SSL (ExPLoRA +8% linear probe on satellite, 2406.10973; FINO metadata-guided, 2606.05107; the RAD-DINO and Midnight examples).
  - Then distil to the deployment model (timm `--kd-*`; 2106.05237).
- **Always report the frozen linear probe next to any fine-tune.** If fine-tuning does not beat it on held-out and shifted splits, ship the probe.

**By domain.**
- **Natural images:** DINOv2-reg or SigLIP 2 (DINOv3 if the licence clears).
- **Dense tasks:** DINOv3, PE-Spatial, TIPSv2 or C-RADIOv4.
- **Chest X-ray:** RAD-DINO (MIT).
- **Pathology with a permissive licence:** Midnight-12k (MIT), then Virchow v1 / H-optimus-0 / Prov-GigaPath and Flash / Hibou (all Apache-2.0 but gated).
- **Pathology research:** UNI2-h, CONCH, Virchow2 and H-optimus-1 lead the benchmarks (2408.15823, 2505.20202).
- **Remote sensing, RGB:** DINOv3-SAT (custom licence) or DINOv2.
- **Remote sensing, multispectral:** Prithvi-EO-2.0 or TerraMind (Apache-2.0), always against a supervised UNet (PANGAEA 2412.04204).
- **Documents:** SigLIP 2 NaFlex or PE-Lang (Apache-2.0).
- **Industrial:** anomalib PatchCore, EfficientAD or Dinomaly with a DINOv2 backbone.

**By compute and latency.**
- **CPU-only:** extract features once at 224 with ViT-S/ConvNeXt-T (about 27 img/s, so about 6 minutes per 10k images), then use scikit-learn. Fine-tune only MobileNetV4-S/M-class CNNs.
- **Single GPU:** LoRA or LP-FT on ViT-B/L. Full fine-tuning of ViT-L at 224 is feasible.
- **Mobile, under 5 ms:** MobileNetV4-Conv-S/M or RepViT; distil from a foundation teacher when accuracy falls short.
- **High-resolution inputs:** CSATv2 (2.9 GMAC at 512), EfficientViT, or C-RADIOv4's ViTDet mode (verify these on the device).

**Sanity rules.**
- ImageNet accuracy predicts transfer well (r = 0.99 for fixed features), but regularisers that raise ImageNet accuracy can hurt feature transfer (1805.08974). Test 2-3 encoders from different families on your own validation split.
- Copy normalisation and resolution from each checkpoint's config (section C). MIM encoders (MAE, BEiT, Hiera) need fine-tuning or attentive probing, not a linear probe.
